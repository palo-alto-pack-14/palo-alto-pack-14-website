  <summary class="text-xl font-bold cursor-pointer text-cub-blue list-none flex items-center justify-between">
    Can we try it out first?
    <svg class="w-5 h-5 transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-width="2" d="M19 9l-7 7-7-7"></path></svg>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-7">Absolutely! We encourage families to attend a meeting or two before committing. Just <a href="https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeZbDPpaONQh3L9yGVodE8sJ8rbmPXoBywZnY17GUBEnvHO-Q/viewform" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="text-cub-blue underline decoration-cub-blue/30 underline-offset-2 hover:decoration-cub-blue">reach out to us</a> and we'll invite you to an upcoming den or Pack meeting so your child can experience Scouting firsthand with no obligation to join.</p>
</details>

<details class="p-6 bg-white rounded-xl ring-1 ring-slate-200 group">
  <summary class="text-xl font-bold cursor-pointer text-cub-blue list-none flex items-center justify-between">
    What is the time commitment?
    <svg class="w-5 h-5 transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-width="2" d="M19 9l-7 7-7-7"></path></svg>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-7">Cub Scouts typically meets once or twice a month for den meetings, plus a monthly Pack meeting. Most meetings run about an hour. There are also optional events throughout the year like camping trips, service projects, and special activities. We understand families are busy, and participation is flexible!</p>
</details>

<details class="p-6 bg-white rounded-xl ring-1 ring-slate-200 group">
  <summary class="text-xl font-bold cursor-pointer text-cub-blue list-none flex items-center justify-between">
    Is Scouting only for boys?
    <svg class="w-5 h-5 transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-width="2" d="M19 9l-7 7-7-7"></path></svg>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-7">No. Scouting America welcomed girls into Cub Scouts in 2018, and girls can participate fully in all ranks from Lion through Arrow of Light. Our Pack welcomes all children in kindergarten through 5th grade, regardless of gender.</p>
</details>

<details class="p-6 bg-white rounded-xl ring-1 ring-slate-200 group">
  <summary class="text-xl font-bold cursor-pointer text-cub-blue list-none flex items-center justify-between">
    What's my role as a parent or guardian?
    <svg class="w-5 h-5 transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-width="2" d="M19 9l-7 7-7-7"></path></svg>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-7">Parental involvement is encouraged and makes Scouting a richer experience for everyone. At minimum, we ask that a parent or guardian accompany their scout to meetings.</p>
</details>

<details class="p-6 bg-white rounded-xl ring-1 ring-slate-200 group">
  <summary class="text-xl font-bold cursor-pointer text-cub-blue list-none flex items-center justify-between">
    How else can parents or guardians get involved?
    <svg class="w-5 h-5 transition-transform group-open:rotate-180" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" stroke-width="2" d="M19 9l-7 7-7-7"></path></svg>
  </summary>
  <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-7">Cub Scouts is a family program, and our Pack runs best when everyone pitches in. Every family plays a role in making the experience fun, organized, and meaningful for the Scouts. Simply put, the Pack cannot operate without volunteers.</p>
  <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-7">We ask parents to support Den Leaders and Pack Leaders by volunteering when opportunities arise. We understand that schedules are busy and it is not always possible to help every time, and that is completely okay. No one is expected to volunteer at every event.</p>
  <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-7">There are many different ways to get involved throughout the year. Some roles are quick and easy, such as helping set up or clean up at a Pack meeting. Other opportunities involve a bigger commitment, such as helping run an event, serving as a Den Leader, or joining the Pack Committee.</p>
  <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-7">If you're ready to lead a den or Pack activity, please talk with our Cubmaster and fill out an <a href="https://tinyurl.com/JoinPack14" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="text-cub-blue underline decoration-cub-blue/30 underline-offset-2 hover:decoration-cub-blue">adult application</a>.</p>
</details>

    <!-- Types of Fees -->
    <h4 class="mt-8 mb-2 text-base font-bold tracking-wide uppercase text-cub-blue">Types of Fees</h4>
    <p>There are two types of fees: registration fees and Pack dues. Scouting America and Pacific Skyline Council registration fees are paid at the time of registration and support the Scouting program at the national, council, and district levels.</p>
    <p class="mt-3">Pack dues are collected at the beginning of each Scouting year and cover the operating costs of our Pack, including each Scout's awards and patches, Den activities, volunteer registration, and expenses for official Pack events. If a Scout joins later in the year, dues are prorated based on the join date.</p>
    <p class="mt-3">We strive to keep costs as low as possible while delivering a fun, engaging, and well-rounded Cub Scout experience.</p>
