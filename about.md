    <p class="mt-4 text-lg leading-7">

    </p>

    <div class="grid gap-12 mt-10">
        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-cub-blue">The Scout Oath</h3>
            <p class="mt-4 text-lg leading-7 italic">
                On my honor I will do my best<br>
                To do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law;<br>
                To help other people at all times;<br>
                To keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight.
            </p>
